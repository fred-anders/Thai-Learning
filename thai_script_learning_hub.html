💡 Crucial Definition: Live vs. Dead Syllables

  • Live Syllables: End with a long vowel or smooth nasal sounds (M, N, NG). They let the air flow continuously.
  • Dead Syllables: End with a short vowel or abrupt stop sounds (P, T, K). They choke the airflow suddenly.
